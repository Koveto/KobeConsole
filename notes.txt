TEAM_SELECT

Shared saved-team selection state.
Supported modes:
"BATTLE"
"TEAMBUILDER"
"IMPORT"
"SAVE"
Battle mode
Always includes Random Team
Select a team before battle
Proceeds to TEAM_PREVIEW
Teambuilder mode
Always includes Create Team
Never includes Random Team
Create, edit, rename, duplicate, and erase teams
Edit a selected Pokémon through editable PKMN_INFO
Import mode
Select a saved team
Select a Pokémon from that team
Copy it into the Damage Calculator
Do not change the saved-team Pokémon
Save mode
Opened from the Damage Calculator
Select a saved team
Select one Pokémon slot
Overwrite that saved Pokémon with the calculator attacker or defender
Do not create teams
Do not remember where the calculator Pokémon originally came from
TEAM_SELECT should be configured centrally by change_state() using pending entry context, rather than relying on stale mode flags from its previous use.

Future revealed-information support

During battle, PKMN_INFO_species can eventually incorporate partial knowledge:

A used move becomes revealed in one moveset slot.
Outspeeding establishes a lower bound for Speed.
Damage dealt establishes a possible offensive-stat range.
Damage received establishes possible HP and defensive-stat ranges.
Nature possibilities narrow as stat ranges narrow.
Revealed values remain ranges unless battle evidence determines an exact value.

Possible future display:

SPA 330-350
HP 300-340
DEF 220-255

This is deferred until battle implementation.
